RESPONSIBLE AI CHECKLIST
========================

1. Confirm dataset ownership.
2. Confirm permission to use data.
3. Review PII columns.
4. Review sensitive columns.
5. Remove or mask restricted fields.
6. Confirm target column is ethical.
7. Check leakage-risk columns.
8. Document limitations and bias.
9. Use human review for high-risk decisions.
10. Do not start ML until governance review is complete.
