
METADATA QUESTIONS FOR USER / DOMAIN EXPERT
==================================================

1. What does each row represent?
2. What does each column mean?
3. Which column is the real target column for ML?
4. Is the dataset real, synthetic, sampled, or modified?
5. Who collected or created this dataset?
6. When was the dataset collected?
7. What is the source of the dataset?
8. Are there privacy restrictions?
9. Are there sensitive columns?
10. Are there columns that must not be used in ML?
11. Are there known errors or biases?
12. What is the intended ML/AI use?
13. What should not be done with this dataset?
14. Are numeric units clearly defined?
15. Are category meanings clearly defined?
16. Are there legal, ethical, or consent limitations?
